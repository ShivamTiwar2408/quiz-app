## Root

- **Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu** — Kṛṣṇa Himself; also called Nimāi / Viśvambhara.

## Pañca-tattva

- **Nityānanda Prabhu** — some say initiated by Mādhavendra Purī, others by Lakṣmīpati Tīrtha.
  - Father: **Hāḍāi Paṇḍita** — gave Nityānanda to a wandering sannyāsī who asked for a brahmacārī assistant, then died in separation.
  - Mother: **Padmāvatī**.
- **Advaita Ācārya** — Mahā-Viṣṇu / Sadāśiva; disciple of Mādhavendra Purī; leader of the brāhmaṇas of Śāntipura; had houses in Navadvīpa and Śāntipura; devotees met at his house before the Lord's appearance to discuss Bhagavad-gītā & Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.
  - Wife: **Sītādevī**.
  - Son: **Raghava Paṇḍita**.
- **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**.
- **Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura** — formerly Nārada Muni; kīrtana was held at his home (Śrīvāsa-aṅgana), where Cāṇḍ Kazi broke the mṛdaṅga; after the Lord took sannyāsa he left Navadvīpa for Halisahara.
  - Four brothers: **Śrīvāsa, Śrī Rāma, Śrīpati, Śrīnidhi**.
  - Wife: **Mālinī**.
  - Niece: **Nārāyaṇī** — mother of **Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura**.

## Guru-paramparā (spiritual lineage & sannyāsa-guru)

- **Lakṣmīpati Tīrtha** → guru of Mādhavendra Purī.
- **Mādhavendra Purī** — guru of Īśvara Purī; disciple of Lakṣmīpati; introduced the concept of conjugal love (and the worship of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa) into the Madhva-Gauḍīya line — before him (up to Lakṣmīpati Tīrtha) only Kṛṣṇa was worshipped.
- **Īśvara Purī** — disciple of Mādhavendra Purī; dīkṣā (initiating) guru of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu.
  - **Govinda** — disciple of Īśvara Purī; became the personal servant of CM.
  - **Kāśīśvara Paṇḍita** — servant of Īśvara Purī (along with Govinda).
- **Paramānanda Purī** — disciple of Mādhavendra Purī; formerly Uddhava; godbrother of Īśvara Purī; dealings with CM were mainly on a parental level.
- **Raṅga Purī** — another disciple of Mādhavendra Purī; CM met him at Paṇḍharpur.
- **Keśava Bhāratī** — sannyāsa-guru of CM; formerly Akrūra / Sāndīpani Muni; of the Śaṅkara-sampradāya, but said to have been initiated (some say into sannyāsa) by Mādhavendra Purī, hence considered a Vaiṣṇava.
- **Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita** — teacher of Caitanya (taught Him Sanskrit grammar).

## Family of Mahāprabhu

- **Upendra Miśra** — great devotee; grandfather of CM; had 7 sons.
- **Jagannātha Miśra** (a.k.a. Purandara) — father of CM; formerly Nanda Mahārāja.
- **Śacīmātā (Śacīdevī)** — mother of CM; formerly Yaśodā; 8 daughters passed away, then Viśvarūpa and CM were born.
- **Viśvarūpa** — 9th child of Śacīdevī; incarnation of Saṅkarṣaṇa; took sannyāsa and became **Śaṅkarāraṇya**.
- **Nīlāmbara Cakravartī** — father of Śacīdevī; formerly Garga Muni.
- **Lakṣmīpriyā** — 1st wife of CM; daughter of Vallabhācārya; formerly Jānakī / Rukmiṇī; died of snakebite (the snake of separation) when the Lord went to East Bengal.
- **Viṣṇupriyā** — 2nd wife of CM; the Goddess of Fortune.
- **Acāryaratna (Candraśekhara)** — husband of Śacīmātā's sister, hence CM's uncle; acted as the father of the Lord when Jagannātha Miśra passed away; CM stayed at his house (and was there during the Cāṇḍ Kazi pastime); present with Nityānanda & Mukunda Datta when CM took sannyāsa.

## Devotee families

- **Bhavānanda Rāya** — father of Rāmānanda Rāya; formerly Pāṇḍu; his 5 sons are likened to the 5 Pāṇḍavas:
  - **Rāmānanda Rāya** — formerly Arjuna / Viśākhā / Lalitā; Governor of South India; kāyastha; gṛhastha.
  - **Vāṇīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka** — given by Bhavānanda as a personal servant to CM.
  - **Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka** — younger brother of Rāmānanda Rāya.
  - **Kalānidhi**.
  - **Sudhānidhi**.
- **Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya** — renowned Vedānta scholar of Purī; formerly an impersonalist, converted by CM (the 7-day talks); elderly householder-teacher whom CM took shelter of like a father; shown the six-armed (ṣaḍ-bhuja) form; his father was a classmate of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī.
  - Wife: **Ṣāṭhīmātā** — mother of Ṣāṭhī.
  - Brother: **Vidyā-Vācaspati**.
  - Brother-in-law: **Gopīnātha Ācārya** — convinced of CM's divinity when Sārvabhauma was unsure; an associate of Mukunda Datta.
- **Vāsudeva Datta** — father of Mukunda Datta; formerly Prahlāda Mahārāja; offered the famous prayer to take on the sins of all living beings.
  - **Mukunda Datta** — classmate and friend of CM; sang for the Lord's pleasure; formerly the Vraja singer Madhukaṇṭha; once rebuked for mixing with māyāvādīs.
- **The three Ghosh brothers** (great devotees & kīrtana singers): **Govinda Ghosh, Vāsudeva Ghosa, Mādhava Ghosa**.

## Earlier Vaiṣṇava poets (whose songs the Lord relished)

- **Jayadeva** — 11th–12th century poet (Gīta-govinda).
- **Vidyāpati** — 14th century poet.
- **Caṇḍīdāsa** — 14th century poet.

## Personal servants of the Lord

- **Kāla Kṛṣṇadāsa** — servant of CM on the South India tour; was allured by the Bhaṭṭathāris.
- **Īśāna** — domestic servant; served the Lord's mother and wife even after His sannyāsa.

## Kings, rulers & officials

- **Pratāparudra Mahārāja** — King of Orissa.
- **Cāṇḍ Kazi** — city magistrate under Nawab Hussain; the mṛdaṅga-breaking / nagara-saṅkīrtana pastime.
- **Nawab Hussain** — King of Bengal.

## Scholars & heads of other lines

- **Vallabhācārya** — head of the Puṣṭi-mārga (Vallabha-sampradāya); the Śrīhars Swami & prostitute pastime.
- **Brahmānanda Bhāratī** — formerly a māyāvādī sannyāsī; CM stopped him from wearing deerskin; godbrother of Īśvara Purī.
- **Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī**.

## Householders of the Navadvīpa & Purī pastimes

- **Buddhimanta Khān** — the Lord's wealthy brāhmaṇa neighbour in Navadvīpa; arranged CM's marriage to Viṣṇupriyā; the "sprinkling water and becoming Muslim" pastime (due to striking the nawab).
- **Vanamālī Ghaṭaka** — formerly Viśvāmitra; arranged the marriage of CM and Lakṣmī.
- **Kāśī Miśra** — CM stayed at his Purī home after the South India tour (near the temple, yet secluded); received darśana of the four-armed form.
- **Tapana Miśra** — a brāhmaṇa from East Bengal; CM first revealed the saṅkīrtana movement to him.
- **Tukārāma** — initiated by CM at Paṇḍharpur (where they worship Viṭhaladeva); later spread saṅkīrtana across Maharashtra.

## Figures of specific pastimes

- **Gopāla Cāpala** — the Bhavānī-pūjā pastime at Śrīvāsa-aṅgana to defame Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura; got leprosy for the offence; rebuked by CM, later forgiven by Śrīvāsa.
- **Rāmadāsa Vipra** — a brāhmaṇa grieved that Sītā had been kidnapped and touched by Rāvaṇa; CM pacified him — it was the māyā (illusory) form that Rāvaṇa touched.
- **Nṛsiṁhānanda Brahmacārī** — mentally decorated the road for the Lord toward Vṛndāvana, but could envision it only as far as Kānāi Nāṭaśālā.
- **Nakula Brahmacārī** — the Lord entered his body.
- **Gopāla Bhaṭṭa** — son of Vyeṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa; served CM when He visited Raṅga-kṣetra.

## Close devotees of Mahāprabhu

(Intimate associates; relationship to the wider circle not further specified in the source.)

- **Haridāsa Ṭhākura** — the nāmācārya; formerly Brahmā (atoned in the Vimohana-līlā) / incarnation of Prahlāda; early association with Advaita Ācārya; residence at Jagannātha Purī was Siddha-bakula.
- **Svarūpa Dāmodara** — formerly Puruṣottama Ācārya of Navadvīpa; took sannyāsa (named "Tīrtha") at Vārāṇasī; examined every writing before it was presented to CM; sang confidential poems for the Lord's pleasure.
- **Jagadānanda Paṇḍita** — formerly Satyabhāmā (a principal queen of Kṛṣṇa in Dvārakā); the full embodiment of the "leftist" (vāma) mood of loving intimacy.
- **Murāri Gupta** — formerly Hanumān; an expert physician; born in Sylhet, came to Navadvīpa; a few years older than CM.
- **Damodara Paṇḍita** — elder brother of Śaṅkara; a very faithful, staunch devotee who would fearlessly point out anything that might touch or taint the Lord's character, regardless of the Lord's exalted position.
- **Śivānanda Sena**.
- **Vakreśvara Paṇḍita**.
- **Rasikānanda Prabhu** — initiated by Śyāmānanda Prabhu.
- **Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya**.
- **Chota Haridāsa**.
- **Devānanda Paṇḍita**.
- **Kamalākānta**.
- **Kholāvecā Śrīdhara**.
- **Nandana Ācārya**.
- **Narahari dāsa (Nandana)**.
- **Pradyumna Miśra**.
- **Puruṣottama Bhaṭṭācārya**.
- **Rāmāi**.
- **Śikhi Māhiti**.
- **Śuklāmbara**.
- **Vanamālī Ācārya**.
- **Vidyānidhi (Vidyā)**.
- **Kāla Kṛṣṇadāsa** (one of the twelve gopālas) — a great devotee (distinct from the servant on the South India tour).
