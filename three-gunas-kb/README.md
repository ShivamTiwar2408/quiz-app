# The Three Guṇas

A first-principles guide to the three modes of material nature — *sattva*, *rajas*, *tamas* — as a working model you can actually read your own state against, rather than a taxonomy to memorise.

**Live:** https://shivamtiwar2408.github.io/quiz-app/three-gunas-kb/guide/index.html

## Contents

**The model**
- Start here: the one idea — your state is a proportion of three forces, never one of them
- What *guṇa* means — quality *and* rope; why both senses are load-bearing (BG 14.5)
- Rope, road, routine — the three mechanisms by which a mode takes hold: outer pull, your choice, inner default
- Sattva — stillness with direction
- Rajas — heat and motion
- Tamas — stillness without direction
- Your state is a point, not a slot — the guṇa triangle, with activity and direction-of-mind as axes
- No mode is bad — the misfire is always timing, not presence
- Why sattva slides into tamas — the adjacency almost everyone gets backwards

**Why aim for sattva**
- Better decisions — including decisions about when to use the other two modes
- Structure frees decision energy — why pre-deciding the recurring protects the novel
- Plans vs. planning — plans are the lane, planning is the direction
- Perfectionism & pessimism — the signature pathology of each lower mode, and pessimism's three targets

**The practice · I·C·U**
- The I·C·U map — three nested scopes, and what happens when they conflict
- I — pause (for AIR), pray, process
- C — choose, commit, cherish (in three tenses)
- U — the times and places already in sattva; tap, then train

**Working with it**
- Getting out of a mode, right now — the two doors out of tamas, and which to use
- Deepening an attraction — attention, association, appreciation
- Don't glamorize or demonize — the third stance, and what "utilize" means
- The crowning result — less inner friction, more outer contribution

**Reference**
- Verses referenced — BG 14.5, 14.18, 2.62, 2.69, each verified against vedabase.io
- Sources & method — provenance, and which two citations are attributed rather than verified
