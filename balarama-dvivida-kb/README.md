# How a Devotee Falls — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.67

A first-principles study guide to the chapter in which Lord Balarāma slays Dvivida, organized around one idea: the fight is the frame, and the content is a causal chain — pride → offence to the Lord's representative → reduced spiritual strength → bad association → *anarthas* → further offence — which the Lord Himself arranged so we could see it step by step.

**Live:** https://shivamtiwar2408.github.io/quiz-app/balarama-dvivida-kb/guide/index.html

Written in the first person, from the class *SB 10.67 Lord Balarama slays Dvivida Gorilla* — Fort Lauderdale, FL, Balarāma-pūrṇimā, August 23rd 2024 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zSgd87is5_E).

## Contents

- **The frame** — the one idea; Parīkṣit's question and where 67 sits after 65 and 66; where we are in cosmic time (14 Manus, Rāma in the 24th yuga-cycle, Kṛṣṇa in the 28th, and why that makes Dvivida a *cirañjīva*)
- **Who Dvivida was** — a *vānara* manufactured to slip Rāvaṇa's benedictions, son of the Aśvinī-kumāras, minister of Sugrīva, one of the five Rāma left behind; his strength measured twice (the jumping contest in yojanas, ten thousand elephants); the *āvaraṇa-devatā* problem and the Jaya–Vijaya parallel
- **The fall** — the two offences against Lakṣmaṇa (one spoken as advice, one only thought); why Lakṣmaṇa specifically, as *guru-tattva*; the mechanism of degradation in Viśvanātha Cakravartī's order, where bad association is the *punishment* for offence rather than its cause; Narakāsura, himself corrupted the same way
- **The līlā** — the rampage on Ānarta; Raivataka, Revatī and *vāruṇī*; the provocation, and why Balarāma threw a rock instead of His club; the fight as reversed escalation, ending bare-handed; killed and therefore liberated (SB 2.7.34–35), with the caution that "we don't imitate"
- **Taking it home** — Balarāma's four protections from *Garga-saṁhitā* mapping each slain demon onto lust, anger, greed, illusion; controlling the tongue (BG 17.15, *prasādam*, the mechanics of speech); anger, the Bhīma–Duryodhana proof case, and why *bhakti* is the cure; where to take a problem, on Balarāma-pūrṇimā
- **Appendices** — the questions on cosmic time (partial vs. total devastation, day and night for Brahmā) and a glossary of the chapter's Sanskrit

## A note on the source

The recording was auto-transcribed, which garbles Sanskrit beyond recognition. Every name, number, text reference and technical term was reconstructed and checked against primary sources. Where the class stopped short — the circumstance of Mainda's fall, how day and night are defined for Brahmā — the guide stops short too, and says so.
