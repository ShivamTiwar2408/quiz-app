# How a Devotee Falls — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.67

Lord Balarāma kills Dvivida the ape. That is the surface. Underneath it is one chain of causes: pride → offence to the Lord's representative → spiritual strength reduced → bad association → *anarthas* → more offences. The Lord arranged the fall so we could watch it happen step by step.

Written in plain language, in the first person, with the stories told in full.

**Live:** https://shivamtiwar2408.github.io/quiz-app/balarama-dvivida-kb/guide/index.html

From the class *SB 10.67 Lord Balarama slays Dvivida Gorilla* — Fort Lauderdale, FL, Balarāma-pūrṇimā, August 23rd 2024 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zSgd87is5_E).

## Contents

- **The frame** — what the chapter is for; Parīkṣit's question, and why he asks it after 65 and 66; where we are in cosmic time (14 Manus, Rāma in the 24th yuga-cycle, Kṛṣṇa in the 28th, and why that makes Dvivida a *cirañjīva*)
- **Who Dvivida was** — the loophole in Rāvaṇa's benedictions and why the demigods fathered monkeys; son of the Aśvinī-kumāras, minister to Sugrīva; the southern search told in full — Aṅgada giving up on the beach, Hanumān refusing to, and Sampāti the vulture who came down the mountain for lunch and wept for his brother instead; the jumping contest; ten thousand elephants; the *āvaraṇa-devatā* problem and the Jaya–Vijaya parallel
- **The fall** — the two offences against Lakṣmaṇa, one said out loud as friendly advice and one only thought; why Lakṣmaṇa, as *guru-tattva*; Viśvanātha Cakravartī's order of causes, where bad association is the *punishment* for the offence and not its cause; Narakāsura, who had Lord Varāha for a father and went the same way
- **The līlā** — the rampage on Ānarta, item by item; Raivataka, Revatī and the *vāruṇī*; the six steps of the provocation, and why Balarāma threw a rock instead of His club; the fight hit by hit, with Dvivida running out of things to throw while Balarāma puts His weapons down; killed and so liberated (SB 2.7.34–35) — with the warning that "we don't imitate"
- **Taking it home** — Balarāma's four protections from *Garga-saṁhitā*, matching each demon He killed to lust, anger, greed and illusion; controlling the tongue (BG 17.15, *prasādam*, the mechanics of speech); anger, Bhīma and Duryodhana unable to stop for their own teacher, and why *bhakti* is the cure; where to take a problem, on Balarāma-pūrṇimā
- **Appendices** — the questions on cosmic time (partial vs. total devastation, day and night for Brahmā) and a glossary of the chapter's Sanskrit

## A note on the source

Where the class stopped short — the circumstance of Mainda's fall, and how day and night are defined for Brahmā — the guide stops short too, and says so.
